A game visual effect sprite on a pure solid black background: a golden egg-summon flash.

Design: a tall vertical oval of brilliant warm gold light shaped like an egg,
a white-hot molten core in the center, three concentric oval shockwave rings
expanding outward at slightly different sizes, thin straight light rays
radiating up and down, small floating golden sparkles and short curved streaks
scattered around the oval, plus a soft outer glow that fades to pure black.

Rendering: additive-glow style, painted with smooth luminous gradients,
no outline, no hard cut edges, high contrast against black,
symmetrical centered composition, the whole effect fits inside the frame with margin.

no text, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no border, no character, no duck,
no egg shell, no landscape, no ground,
the entire background must stay pure black so it can be used as an additive glow layer.

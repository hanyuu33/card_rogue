A full-bleed fantasy card illustration of one golden duck egg flying through the air,
caught in the split second right after it was thrown, painted as a trading-card artwork.

COMPOSITION
The golden egg is the single subject, seen from a low three-quarter angle, tilted about 25 degrees
as if spinning in flight, its long axis running from lower-left to upper-right.
It sits slightly above the vertical centre and stays entirely inside the upper three quarters of the frame
(the bottom of the artwork may be cropped when the card is displayed in game).
Behind and below the egg: a short trail of golden sparks, embers and dust, plus three thin speed streaks
curving away toward the lower-left, showing where it came from.
The egg casts no ground shadow, it is airborne, high above the battlefield.
Background is a dusk battlefield, out of focus: dark wooden palisade stakes, torn banners,
dust haze and a few drifting embers, deep brown and muted olive, low contrast.
Leave breathing room around the egg; nothing touches the frame edges.

THE EGG
A large smooth duck egg, clearly taller than wide, polished metallic gold shell:
warm creamy highlight on the upper left, deep amber to burnt orange along the lower right,
a thin engraved golden ring band near the top, a faint embossed baby-duck silhouette on the front face,
three or four sharp sparkle glints. Solid gold, precious, a little magical.

STYLE
Thick dark brown outline, flat cel shading with two or three tone steps,
warm gold and amber against dark brown and olive,
strong rim light on the egg so the silhouette pops, painted fantasy game art,
high contrast so it stays readable when shrunk down to a small unit card.

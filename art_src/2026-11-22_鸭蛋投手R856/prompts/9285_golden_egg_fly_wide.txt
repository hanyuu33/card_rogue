A wide landscape fantasy card illustration of one golden duck egg flying through the air,
caught in the split second right after it was thrown, painted as a trading-card artwork.

KEEP FROM THE REFERENCE
The exact same egg design: polished metallic gold shell, warm creamy highlight upper left,
deep amber to burnt orange on the lower right, a thin engraved golden ring band near the top,
a simple embossed baby-duck silhouette on the front face, three or four sharp sparkle glints,
thick dark brown outline and flat cel shading. Same colour scheme. Only the framing changes.

FRAMING (most important)
Zoom out to a wide shot: the egg is only about one third of the image height,
roughly one fifth of the image width, and sits with its centre in the middle third of the frame.
It must stay well clear of all four edges, with lots of open sky around it.
The egg is tilted 25 degrees, spinning, long axis from lower-left to upper-right.
A thin curved trail of golden sparks and dust streams out behind it toward the lower left.

SCENE
Dusk battlefield far below and behind: a dark wooden palisade, torn banners, drifting dust and embers,
the horizon line in the lower third, all slightly out of focus.
Above is open warm dusk sky with soft clouds, lit from the upper left.
The egg casts no ground shadow; it is high in the air.

STYLE
Painted fantasy game art, thick dark brown outline, flat cel shading with two or three tone steps,
warm gold and amber against a dusky brown and olive backdrop,
strong rim light on the egg so its silhouette pops at small size.

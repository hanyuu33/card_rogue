## 9237 巴兰-α（重装突击 · 5 攻 / 250 血）

- 参照图（可选）：`assets/pics/9223.png`（执法者）—— 只借「人形机械 + 军灰装甲 + 细黑线赛璐璐」的画法，不借它的制服、步枪、机库构图。
- 建议参数：1536×1024 / 3:2，`input_fidelity=low`。

```
JAPANESE ANIME trading-card illustration, modern light-fantasy mobile game card art, anime
cel-shaded style, clean crisp THIN black line art, 2-step cel shading with real volume and depth,
HARD-EDGED shadows, hand-painted look. NO photorealistic rendering, NO ray-traced reflections,
NO photographic metal texture, NO cinematic 3D render, NO depth-of-field bokeh, NO flat vector
illustration, NO painterly airbrushing.
dark understated palette, single accent colour limited to one element, low-saturation, clean
silhouette against a dark background, the subject is the brightest sharpest thing in the frame.
NO neon explosion, NO full-screen glow, NO muddy brown palette, NO western comic cross-hatching.

SQUARE CARD ILLUSTRATION for an enemy boss card named "Barran-alpha".

SUBJECT: ONE single HUMANOID heavy assault war-machine, a soldier-shaped biped built out of
SOVIET-STYLE TANK PARTS. 7-heads-tall, broad and top-heavy, standing on two legs.
HEAD: a squat CAST-STEEL TURRET dome used as a helmet, faceless, with a single narrow horizontal
vision slit glowing pale cyan, a small commander's cupola ring on top with periscope blocks.
SHOULDERS: two massive SLOPED FRONT-HULL ARMOUR plates worn as pauldrons, thick cast steel with
bead welds and rows of rivets, a few explosive-reactive-armour bricks bolted on.
LEFT ARM: a short thick 122mm gun barrel with a double-baffle muzzle brake used as a battering
ram, hydraulic cables hanging from it. RIGHT ARM: a huge blocky cast-steel fist.
CHEST: a diesel engine radiator grille with heat shimmer, welded seams, a single stencilled
Greek letter ALPHA on the shoulder plate crossed out with one swipe of red paint.
LEGS: exposed ROAD WHEELS on the outer calves and TRACK LINKS worn as greaves, wide flat slab feet.
FINISH: deep olive-drab army green paint, faded and chipped, white winter-wash streaks, mud and
powder soot, rust weeping from bolts. The ONLY glowing element is the pale cyan vision slit.
POSE: slowly pressing forward, weight braced, gun-arm raised slightly, an unstoppable wall.
BACKGROUND: dark cold industrial hangar, ruined concrete, thin cold rim light.

NO other machines, NO people, NO human face, NO visible eyes, NO anime girl, NO mecha-girl,
NO animal, NO readable text, NO words, NO flag, NO real military insignia, NO logo, NO watermark,
NO card frame, NO border.
```

- 检查点：① 是不是**人形**（两条腿站着）；② 炮塔盔 / 履带护腿 / 倾斜肩甲至少三样能一眼认出；
③ 全图只有观察缝在发光；④ 没有脸。
